Tax Advisory

Where Strategy Replaces Guesswork

Most CPAs work from your rearview mirror — telling you what you owe after the year is already closed. Advisory works the other direction: we look at what's ahead and shape it before it's locked in.

The Difference
Tax Preparation

Reports What Already Happened

A tax return documents decisions that were made months earlier. It keeps you compliant, but by the time it's filed, there's nothing left to change.

Tax Assessment

Looks At What's Still Ahead

Before the year closes, there's still room to act — how income is structured, when a gain is realized, which entity makes sense. That window is where the real savings live.

Tax Advisory

Connects the Two, Year-Round

Advisory is the throughline. Preparation and assessments become checkpoints inside an ongoing strategy — not one-off events you start over every spring.

What's Included
  • A dedicated advisor who knows your full financial picture — not a call center.
  • Strategies get implemented, not just recommended — we execute alongside you.
  • Your plan evolves as your income, entities, and investments change.
  • Quarterly check-ins keep the strategy current, not just an annual conversation.
  • If a strategy we built draws IRS attention, we're the ones who show up.
Who This Is For

Most clients earn $400K or more annually.

High Tax Liability

Paying $150K or more in federal tax each year and suspecting there's room you haven't captured.

Business Ownership

Running an S-corp, LLC, partnership, or multiple entities where income shifts enough to need quarterly planning.

W-2 or Business Owner ($400K+)

Earning $400K+ through some mix of salary, equity, bonuses, or deferred comp — often across more than one state.

Major Liquidity Events

Selling a business, exercising equity, or realizing a large gain — where the tax decision has to be right the first time.
